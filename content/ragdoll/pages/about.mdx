This is a short English fan site for **Ragdoll Sandbox**, Roblox universe **10771155665**, built as a static site for **ragdollsandbox.wiki**.

## Disclaimer

ragdollsandbox.wiki is an unofficial fan-made guide for *Ragdoll Sandbox*. It is **not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Roblox Corporation, the Rise To Top group, or the people who develop the experience**. All game content, names, artwork, and trademarks belong to their respective owners.

This site does not sell Robux, redeem codes on your behalf, or recover accounts. A code can expire after the description changes. The [codes](/codes) page is a dated copy of the string the game page printed, plus the click-path Dexerto and Roblox Den described. The [guide](/guide) quotes the play pitch from that same description.

Nothing here is account support. Rise To Top’s public group is https://www.roblox.com/groups/257996659/Rise-To-Top. That group is theirs, not this wiki’s mailbox.

<Callout kind="note" title="What “checked” means">
“Checked 6 Nov 2026” means the Roblox games API, the votes API, the thumbnail API, the place page, and the linked articles were read that day. It does not mean someone redeemed SANDBOX in a client for this build.
</Callout>

## Images

The icon, the favicon, and the six gameplay frames are official Roblox thumbnails for universe 10771155665, downloaded from the thumbnail CDN and served from this site so pages do not hotlink Roblox. The file list and the temporary CDN URLs are in [ATTRIBUTION.txt](/images/official/roblox/ATTRIBUTION.txt).

## Privacy

The site is static. It has no accounts and does not ask for your Roblox login.

`analytics.gaId` is empty. The base layout does not load Google Analytics.

The Adsterra Native Banner slot named `afterQuickAnswer` is enabled with this site’s own Get Code for ragdollsandbox.wiki. Zone id `2049d33861ff600d03422ca8d3b161fa` loads from `https://bauval.org/21/2049d33861ff600d03422ca8d3b161fa` on the homepage and guide pages. The container id is `container-2049d33861ff600d03422ca8d3b161fa`. `invokeBase` is empty because the full script URL is set. No advertising script from another game is included. Adult and sensitive categories stay off. The 404 page does not render a slot.

The same privacy note is in the footer of every page, under the heading Privacy. The disclaimer paragraph in that footer is the short form of this page.

## What this site leaves unpublished

- A second redeem code. The 6 Nov 2026 description still names only SANDBOX, even though upvotes were 6,350 and the text says the next code is at 5k likes.
- The six Zombie Rush weapon names, shop prices, and any event end date. They were not in the description.
- A confirmed Discord invite. Guides mention one. The group record did not hand this site a URL it could verify, so none is linked.
- A Google Analytics id. The field stays empty until one is issued for this domain.
